Component diagram

The parts and how they talk

Two programs. The browser extension runs the 3D theater inside the page: it plays the video, delays the picture and the sound together, and sends the app small copies of each frame. The app captures the virtual monitor (where the browser is full screen), works out depth, and draws both eyes into the glasses. They talk over one local WebSocket, 127.0.0.1:47391.

Deciding when the glasses show the virtual monitor

AppController.Evaluate runs on every display change, settings save, theater open or close, and pause. It never changes the display layout itself; it only reacts to it.

Kept frames cost 1.5 bytes a pixel at the size shown (2 s of 1080p at 60 fps is about 373 MB, capped at 640 MB). A video the page may not read (another site's, without CORS) and DRM video play live, with depth from the screen.

Sequence diagram

One frame, from the video to the glasses

With the 2 s buffer. The same frame takes two paths: a small copy to the model straight away, and the full picture held in the page until its sound is heard.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant V as Video element
  participant P as Player (content.js)
  participant D as App: PagePictures + DepthService
  participant R as App: render thread
  participant G as Glasses
  V->>P: presents the frame at 12.500 s (t = 0)
  P->>D: picture: line 3, 12.500, JPEG 518×294
  P->>P: keep the frame in GPU memory, stamp the audio clock
  D->>D: model 9–17 ms, look-ahead hold, post about 3 ms
  D-->>R: depth for (3, 12.500) filed
  Note over P,R: its sound is still in the page's delay line
  P->>P: t = 2.0 s: the sound is heard, draw the kept frame
  P->>R: frame: count n, 12.500, line 3, display time
  R->>R: the capture shows it: take depth (3, 12.500), warp
  R->>G: both eyes, the player's controls flat
Protocol 3

Messages between the page and the app

JSON over the WebSocket, relayed by background.js. Nothing says what you watch: no titles, addresses or caption text. Defined in BrowserLink.Handle and in content.js / background.js.

MessageWayCarries
hellopage → appProtocol number, extension version, browser name. Any protocol but 3 is refused with a tray message to reload.
mediapage → appEach video or audio element: playing, audible, size, whether its sound can be delayed (or why not), how late it plays now.
theaterbothPage: the player opened, moved or closed, its picture in CSS px, px ratio, whether the viewport is the screen. App: open or close it (tray).
framepage → appEach frame the player draws: count, time in the video, line, when it is shown on the app's clock.
picturepage → appEach frame the video presents, as JPEG at the model's input size, with its line and time.
statepage → appPaused, seeking, seek count, live stream, whether it sends pictures, clock error.
overlayspage → appBoxes of the bar, panel, spinner (drawn flat) and caption (held in front), with when they appeared.
commandpage → appA 3D panel button: depth, plane, model, resolution, timing, stretch, video layout.
clockbothNTP-style ping and answer, so frame times are on the app's clock.
statusapp → pageGlasses in 3D (the button shows only then), the panel's values, the model's input size for pictures.
delayapp → pageHow late to play the tab's sound, and the picture (without the trim and output latency).
pingapp → pageEvery 20 s, so the browser keeps the service worker awake.
